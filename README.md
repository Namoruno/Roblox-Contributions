# Roblox Development Portfolio

This repository contains a curated selection of scripts and systems I've developed for various Roblox games. The focus is on showcasing clean, efficient code that solves common game development challenges in the Roblox ecosystem.

## Anti-Cheat Solutions

### Bandaid Anti-Cheat
A lightweight yet effective suite of tools combatting common exploits in Roblox games. It provides protection against remote event manipulation, chatbots, speed hacks, as well as economy surveillance. Bandaid raises the barrier to cheating to require more dedication from cheaters, and is a minimal yet effective solution to games with smaller playerbases.

## Game Development

### Incorporate
https://www.roblox.com/games/17824624898/Incorporate
This is my diamond in the rough.
It took me a couple weeks to conceptualize, a summer to code, and a month to balance.
You collect businesses that stay with you forever,
and you can (will be able to) trade them with other players.
You gain gold by defeating other players in the open world where you can place the businesses
You must strategically place them and purchase land where there are more residents or desirable buildings.
You can upgrade your businesses to increase their income, and monopolizing your industries by maxxing out 5 buildings wins the round.
I still own this game because it's the last game I worked on, and selling it for a few hundred dollars
in typical roblox fashion got old. I love it and I'm proud of it in all it's blocky, pixelated glory.
